# Every Entry's Journey: status history from Entries and Handoffs — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** A person opening any entry (Entries screen) or any handover (Handoffs screen) sees its whole journey as one timeline with who and when at every step, always current: entered → (car handed to branch) → branch handed to area → area sent to the deputy → deputy validated / returned (reason) / resent as version N → collector received (amount, short/over) → disputed / settled → deposited at the bank (reference) → matched to the bank statement. Or, for an area batch: uploaded → approved / rejected (reason) → corrected and resent → approved → collector received → deposited.

**Spec:** the user, 2026-10-05: "the user can check the status of his entries from the entries screen; once he clicks on an entry he should know the full history: when he submitted, when it was sent to the deputy, when the deputy took action, when the collector received, when the collector deposited, so the status is always updated and he can check easily, from the entries screen and from the handoff screen too."

**Architecture:** A read-only server action `getJourney {entryId | handoffId | batchId}` walks the links that already exist and returns ordered steps; nothing new is stored. Links: entry → `consumedBy` (handoff or batch id) and `batchId`; handoff → `sourceEntryIds`/`sourceHandoffIds` (down) and the handoff whose sources contain it (up: `consumedBy` on the handoff); `resubmitOf`/`resubmittedAs`/`history` (versions); batch → `entryIds`, `resultHandoffIds`, `history`; deposit → `kind:'deposit'` with `sourceHandoffIds`; reconciliation → `reconciled`, `reconciledAt`. Timestamps already on rows: `createdAt`, `confirmedAt`, `deputyValidatedAt`, `deputyReturnedAt`, `resolvedAt`, `voidedAt`, `reconciledAt`, batch `approvedAt`/`rejectedAt` (check names in the code; use what exists).

## Global Constraints
- Read-only; scoped exactly like the screens: a person sees the journey of an entry/handover they can already see (`listEntries`/`listHandoffs` rules); steps beyond their scope still show the step (e.g. "received by the collector") but not other branches' amounts.
- One timeline component used in both places (`journeyView_(steps)`): vertical rail, a dot per step (done = green tick, current = pulsing, waiting = hollow, returned/disputed/voided = red), the actor's name, the date and time in the app's language, amounts with `money()`, the reason under returned/disputed/voided steps, the next expected step shown greyed ("Waiting for the collector to receive it").
- Every string ar/en/ur; RTL rail on the right in ar/ur; works at 390px.
- Never a bare `<tr>` through `el()`; `fillTemplate_` for repeated placeholders.
